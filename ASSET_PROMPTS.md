# Wedding theme asset prompts

Use these prompts to create the optional visual assets under `public/theme/`. Keep the screen area of the television transparent or a flat neutral gray so the website can place the live image, static, date, and scan lines over it. Use straight-on product views, no text, no logos, and transparent backgrounds where noted.

## Vintage television — `tv.webp`

Photorealistic front-facing 1960s tabletop CRT television, compact rounded gold and dark walnut cabinet, softly worn polished wood, convex glass screen with a perfectly flat neutral gray screen area for replacement, one round antique brass tuning knob and one smaller knob on the right, tidy horizontal speaker grille, small feet, centered symmetric product view, soft studio lighting, crisp tactile detail, transparent background, no people, no brand, no lettering, no perspective tilt.

## Kraft envelope — `kraft-envelope.webp`

Photorealistic handmade kraft-paper wedding envelope, warm tan cotton paper with subtle fibers, open pointed flap and visible inner fold, straight-on view, soft studio shadow, transparent background, no text, no stamp, no hands.

## Postcard paper texture — `postcard-texture.webp`

Seamless warm ivory handmade postcard paper texture, subtle fine cotton fibers and faint age-softened edges, even diffuse light, flat front view, no writing, no objects, no vignette.

## Postage stamp — `stamp.webp`

Small antique Indian wedding postage stamp illustration, restrained burgundy and antique gold border, delicate engraved floral motif with a simple ring illustration, front-facing, transparent background, no legible lettering.

## Postmark — `postmark.webp`

Vintage circular postmark in faded burgundy ink, delicate concentric circles and tiny decorative marks, front-facing rubber-stamp impression, transparent background, no readable words or dates.

## Wax seal — `wax-seal.webp`

Photorealistic deep crimson sealing wax medallion, gently irregular round edge, embossed intertwined initials P and D without other lettering, soft warm highlights, straight-on, transparent background.

## Brass pin — `pin.webp`

Single small antique brass map pin, rounded polished head and short fine point, straight-on product view, warm studio highlight, transparent background, no shadow beyond a faint contact shadow.

## Photo-booth strip frame — `photo-strip-frame.webp`

Photorealistic vintage photo-booth contact strip, narrow warm-white border with four equal blank portrait windows, slight paper grain and softly aged edges, straight-on, transparent background, no text, no photos.

## Theatre curtain — `curtain-texture.webp`

Seamless deep crimson velvet theatre curtain, close-up vertical pleats with rich dark folds, very low sheen, soft frontal warm light, no tassels, no objects, no text, tileable texture.

## Image notes

- Save optimized WebP files at 2x their display size; keep `tv.webp` under 250 KB and each small decor asset under 100 KB.
- Do not put letters, dates, or names in the generated television screen. The invitation renders those elements as accessible website text.
- The CSS-drawn television and paper textures are usable placeholders until the optional assets are supplied.
